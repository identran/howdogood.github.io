60 Random Acts of Kindness Ideas

Not sure where to start? Here is every act of kindness in our generator, grouped by theme. Most cost nothing and take just a few minutes — pick one and do it today. Or let the generator surprise you.

Kindness for strangers & everyday moments

  • Compliment a stranger on something you genuinely admire about them
  • Hold the door open for someone
  • Leave a generous tip for your server
  • Let someone go ahead of you in line
  • Pay for the coffee of the person behind you in line
  • Write a positive comment on a blog or article you enjoyed
  • Listen attentively to someone without interrupting
  • Share your umbrella with a stranger on a rainy day
  • Write a genuine note of appreciation to someone whose work often goes unnoticed and give it to them directly
  • Smile and make eye contact with people you pass on the street
  • Offer to take a photo for tourists or groups struggling with selfies
  • Leave quarters at a laundromat for someone who might need them
  • Share helpful resources or articles with someone who could benefit from them
  • Say 'please' and 'thank you' more often and truly mean it
  • Bring coffee or treats to your coworkers
  • Return shopping carts to the store entrance
  • Offer your seat to someone on public transportation
  • Give genuine compliments to service workers (cashiers, servers, etc.)
  • Give someone your full attention when they're speaking to you
  • Leave a book you've enjoyed in a public place for someone else to discover

Kindness for your community

  • Leave a positive review for a small business you appreciate
  • Buy a meal for someone experiencing homelessness
  • Donate clothes you no longer wear to a shelter
  • Offer to help an elderly neighbor with groceries or yard work
  • Volunteer at a local food bank
  • Make a donation to a cause you believe in
  • Start a fundraiser for a cause you care about
  • Offer to walk a neighbor's dog
  • Tutor a student who is struggling with a subject you know well
  • Donate blood at your local blood bank
  • Pick up groceries for an elderly or disabled neighbor
  • Mentor someone younger in your profession or hobby
  • Support local artists by purchasing their work or attending their shows
  • Share your knowledge by writing a helpful blog post or tutorial
  • Buy from local farmers markets instead of big chains
  • Volunteer to read to children at a local library or school
  • Share your skills by teaching a free workshop or class

Kindness for the people in your life

  • Send a text to someone you haven't spoken to in a while
  • Write a thank you note to someone who has positively impacted your life
  • Share a book that changed your perspective with someone
  • Forgive someone who hurt you, even if just in your heart
  • Send flowers to someone going through a difficult time
  • Create a care package for a friend who's feeling down
  • Teach someone a skill you're good at
  • Practice patience with someone who is frustrating you
  • Compliment a coworker on their recent accomplishment
  • Call a family member you haven't spoken to in a while
  • Spend quality time with a loved one without your phone
  • Create a playlist for someone based on their music taste
  • Apologize sincerely to someone you've wronged
  • Send an encouraging message to someone going through a tough time

Kindness for the planet

  • Pick up litter in your local park
  • Plant a tree or donate to a reforestation project
  • Reduce your water usage by taking shorter showers this week
  • Reduce single-use plastic in your daily routine
  • Start a community garden
  • Bring reusable bags to the grocery store and encourage others to do the same
  • Feed birds or squirrels in your local park
  • Organize a neighborhood cleanup event
  • Repair something instead of throwing it away

Want a nudge every day?

Visit the How Do Good? generator for a random act of kindness whenever you need one — and share it with a friend who could use a little inspiration.
