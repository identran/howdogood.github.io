How it works

  1. Click an order button above and send us the pre-filled email with your listing details.
  2. We review your listing to make sure it aligns with our values (usually within 2 business days).
  3. Once approved, you'll receive a PayPal invoice. Your listing goes live as soon as payment clears.
  4. Your listing renews monthly — cancel anytime with a single email.

We reserve the right to decline any listing that doesn't align with our ethical values — that curation is exactly what makes this page valuable to advertisers and readers alike. Payment is handled securely through PayPal; no payment details are ever stored on this site.
